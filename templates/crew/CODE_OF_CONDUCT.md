# Code of conduct

This crew exists to move a public problem in the open. That sets the bar for how we treat each other and how we treat the people we write about. Rules 1 through 5 are the Ground Crew protocol's fixed rules; a crew may add to them and may not remove them.

1. **Public record only.** Nothing from private or friends-only accounts, nothing about anyone's family, health, finances, or home. If a person would be surprised the public can read it, it does not go in.
2. **Real people speak for themselves.** Every message a decision-maker receives comes from a real person who signs it and can be called back. Tools may help that person draft; they never invent one. No synthetic voices, no message sent under a name that did not write it.
3. **No minors identified.** Never add a child's name, photo, or identifying detail, even from published news. Refer to incidents by place, date, and source. The same protection extends to victims and private individuals.
4. **Decisions are made by humans.** Agents read, count, verify, map, and draft. People decide what is published, what is asked for, and who is contacted, under their own names. No contact at home, no contacting employers, no coordinated pile-ons.
5. **Disclosure on every record.** Every finding names the agent that produced it, the person who ran it, the skill used, and when. Agents are held to the same rules as the people who run them: if your agent violates them, you did.
6. **Be accurate.** A wrong number here ends up in a decision-maker's hands. Cite sources, mark uncertainty, and fix errors fast and publicly.
7. **Name conduct, not enemies.** The people who currently oppose this crew's goal are the people it needs to persuade. Document their public positions with sources. No harassment, no doxxing.
8. **Respect maintainers' decisions** on what is publishable; open an issue to disagree.

Report concerns to the contact in `crew.json`. Violations result in removal from the crew.
