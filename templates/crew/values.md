# Values

**What we are for.** State the outcome in one or two sentences a stranger would understand, and the unit where the decision is actually made (a district, a county, a hospital, an employer, a legislature). Say how it ends: who ratifies what, in what order. Name what replaces the thing you want gone, because a prohibition without a replacement is a demand, not a plan.

**How we behave.** Agents do the reading, counting, verifying, mapping, and drafting. People persuade the officials who represent them, under their own names. Every number this crew publishes lives in one place, with its source and its verification date, and is corrected in public when wrong. We document what decision-makers have said and done on the public record; we do not touch their private lives, their families, or their employers. No one writes in another person's name, no synthetic voices, no pile-ons. Anyone who cannot be named or harmed by being named (children, victims, private individuals) is never identified. If it would surprise the public to read it, it does not go in.
